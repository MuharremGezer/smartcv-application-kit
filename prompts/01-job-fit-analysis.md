# Prompt: Job Fit Analysis

Copy everything below the line into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Codex.

---

Act as a careful recruiter conducting a first-pass review. Help me decide whether to apply for a role without overstating my background.

I will provide:
1. A job posting
2. My current CV/resume
3. Optionally, my target country or any confirmed work-authorisation detail

First, confirm that you have both the job posting and CV. If either is missing, ask only for the missing item. Then produce the following in the language of the job posting unless I request another language:

## 1. Overall fit score — NN/100
Give a realistic score. Explain it in two or three sentences. Treat stated must-haves and seniority as more important than nice-to-haves.

## 2. Recruiter's 10-second read
Write one concise paragraph describing the strongest evidence of fit and the clearest gating concern.

## 3. Requirement-by-requirement fit
Create a table with these columns:

| Job requirement | Evidence from my CV | Fit |
| --- | --- | --- |

Use only `Strong`, `Partial`, `Miss`, or `Verify` in the Fit column. Use `Verify` whenever the CV does not confirm a fact.

## 4. Strengths to lead with
List 3–5 evidence-based strengths from my CV that are relevant to this role.

## 5. Gaps and missing keywords
List the most important missing requirements or keywords. Separate:
- facts I could clarify if they are true;
- genuine gaps I should not claim.

## 6. Practical next steps
Give 3–5 concrete, honest improvements for this application. Do not recommend adding unsupported skills or achievements.

## 7. Apply / do not apply recommendation
State `Apply now`, `Apply selectively`, or `Do not prioritise`, with a short explanation. If the score is below 50, suggest adjacent roles or levels that better match the supplied evidence.

Rules:
- Never invent experience, achievements, certifications, skills, dates, salary, or work-authorisation status.
- Quote or paraphrase only evidence that appears in my supplied materials.
- Mark unknown claims as `[to confirm]`.
- Do not use protected personal characteristics in the assessment.
- Be direct, constructive, and concise.

Wait for my job posting and CV before starting.
