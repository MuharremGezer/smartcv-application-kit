# Screening Report Spec

Create a simulated senior-recruiter first-pass screen as a `.docx`. Be honest, specific, and constructive.

## Required sections

1. **Title block** — “Recruiter Screening Report,” candidate name if supplied, role/organization, and benchmark level.
2. **Overall Match Score: NN/100** — color-coded: green ≥70, blue 58–69, amber 45–57, red <45; add a brief verdict.
3. **Recruiter's 10-second read** — one concise paragraph and an honest verdict that states gating issues plainly.
4. **Requirement-by-requirement fit** — a 3-column table: Posting requirement | Candidate evidence | Fit. Use Strong, Partial, Miss, or Verify.
5. **What works in the candidate's favor** — 4–6 evidence-based bullets.
6. **Top missing keywords** — up to five exact terms an ATS or recruiter is likely to seek that the supplied materials do not evidence.
7. **Three 10-second red flags** — the most material issues visible at first glance, such as seniority mismatch, missing core experience, unsupported tools, missing metrics, or eligibility uncertainty.
8. **Eligibility checkpoint** — include only if the posting has a material work-authorization, location, clearance, licensing, or scheduling requirement that the candidate has not confirmed.
9. **How to improve for this role** — practical, high-leverage actions that do not ask the candidate to misrepresent themselves.
10. **Better-fit alternatives** — include for a low score (roughly <50) and suggest adjacent roles, teams, or levels grounded in the candidate's evidence.

End with a one-line disclaimer that this is a simulated screen for guidance only.

## Scoring rubric

- Benchmark against the stated seniority. Materially discount a candidate whose demonstrated scope does not meet the level.
- Let stated must-haves dominate the score; a missing core requirement can cap an otherwise strong fit.
- Treat evidence, relevant outcomes, and directly applicable tools as more important than general potential.
- Treat missing quantified outcomes as a fixable weakness, not proof of poor performance.
- Never infer eligibility, work authorization, or a credential; mark it Verify until the candidate confirms it.

## Styling

- Use a clear professional document style: readable 11pt body, distinct headings, and restrained color.
- Make the score prominent and color-coded.
- Use a high-contrast table header and visually distinct fit cells.
- Use a clearly labeled callout for eligibility or other gating checks.
- Add a footer with report name and page number.
