# Cover / Motivation Letter Spec

Deliver a `.docx` in the posting's language. Default length: cover letter ≈250–350 words; motivation letter ≈400–600 words, unless local norms or the posting require otherwise.

## Principles

- Match the posting's language, formality, and organizational tone.
- Use only candidate-provided facts. Ask or add a `[to confirm]` placeholder when a required fact is missing.
- Open with a specific reason for interest and immediate evidence of fit; avoid generic filler.
- Use accurate ownership verbs that reflect the candidate's true contribution.
- Connect one or two concrete experiences to the role's core requirements using the posting's vocabulary.
- Include one distinctive, evidence-based combination of strengths when it helps differentiate the candidate.
- Address a genuine career pivot directly and constructively when it is material to the role.
- Mention work authorization, relocation, or other eligibility only when the candidate has confirmed the wording.
- Close confidently and professionally in the appropriate language.

## Structure

1. **Opening** — specific interest in the role/organization plus a truthful one-line fit hook.
2. **Evidence** — one or two experience paragraphs mapped to core requirements with concrete outcomes or placeholders.
3. **Fit and motivation** — why this organization or mission, distinctive strengths, and any relevant confirmed practical information.
4. **Close** — confident contribution statement and standard sign-off.

Offer an alternate tone only when it would be useful, such as formal versus warmer.
