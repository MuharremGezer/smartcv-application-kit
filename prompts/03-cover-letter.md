# Prompt: Write a Tailored Cover Letter

Copy everything below the line into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Codex.

---

Write a truthful, specific cover letter or motivation letter for a role I am applying to.

I will provide:
1. The job posting
2. My CV/resume or a factual summary of my experience
3. Any confirmed information about the recipient, work authorisation, relocation, or availability that I want mentioned
4. Whether I want a cover letter (about 250–350 words) or motivation letter (about 400–600 words)

First, confirm that you have the job posting and my background. Ask only for information that is essential and missing. Use the job posting's language and tone unless I request another language.

Write the letter with this structure:
1. A specific opening that names the role and gives a truthful fit hook.
2. One or two paragraphs connecting my real experience to the role's most important requirements.
3. A concise paragraph about motivation and relevant strengths.
4. A confident, professional close.

After the letter, add an `Items to confirm` section only if you used placeholders such as `[to confirm]`.

Rules:
- Never invent an employer, result, metric, tool, credential, personal connection, or motivation.
- Do not state that I am authorised to work, willing to relocate, or available on a date unless I explicitly confirm it.
- Avoid generic praise and empty claims such as “I am the perfect candidate.”
- Use concrete evidence from my supplied materials.
- Do not include sensitive personal details unless I explicitly ask.
- Make the result ready to edit and send, but remind me to verify all facts.

Wait for my materials before writing.
